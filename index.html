07 — PHASE 2 · TRANSFER LEARNING

TRANSFER, DON'T RETRAIN.

01

START PRETRAINED

Load the CNN from MATLAB's Spectrum Sensing with Deep Learning example — it already reads 5G/LTE spectrograms.

02

FREEZE THE BACKBONE

Early convolutional blocks keep their weights: generic spectral edge & texture detectors transfer as-is.

03

SWAP THE HEAD

New fully-connected + softmax layers for our classes — wired visually in Deep Network Designer.

04

TRAIN GENTLY

Adam solver, initial learn rate 1e-4 — small steps protect the pretrained features.

05

JUDGE HONESTLY

80 / 10 / 10 split — train, validation, hold-out. Read the confusion matrix, not the loss curve.

WLANBLEW+BNOISE WLAN98110 BLE19720 W+B23941 NOISE10099
